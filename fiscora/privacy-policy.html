Fiscora Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fiscora collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Fiscora mobile app and related public pages. Fiscora is operated by Rejuvenize, an independent software project and development team name used for this app. Rejuvenize is not presented as an incorporated company or registered business entity in this policy. If you have questions about this policy, you can contact rejuvenize@proton.me.

Information We Collect

Fiscora is designed as a local-first finance tracker. We do not require you to create an account to use the app.

  • Financial records you enter, such as accounts, budgets, transactions, and settings, are primarily stored on your device.
  • If you enable app features such as reminders, app lock, export, restore, subscriptions, or rewarded access, related device or service data may be processed to support those features.
  • If you choose to attach or select images inside the app, those images are accessed from your device for the feature you used.
  • If you contact us directly, we may receive the information you provide in your message.

How We Use Information

  • To provide core finance tracking features and store your preferences on your device.
  • To enable optional security, reminder, backup, restore, subscription, and rewarded-access features.
  • To maintain app stability, diagnose crashes, and improve performance.
  • To understand general product usage through privacy-conscious analytics when enabled.
  • To respond to support requests, bug reports, or other messages you send to us.

Local Storage and Device Data

Fiscora is built so your finance data stays local to your device unless you choose to export or share it. We do not operate a user cloud account or sync service for your financial data. Device-local security data, such as app lock settings or credentials stored through secure storage, may remain separate from exported files.

Exported or restored files are handled at your direction. You are responsible for how you store, share, or back up any files you export from the app.

Third-Party Services

Fiscora uses third-party providers to support certain app functions. These providers may process limited technical or service-related data according to their own terms and privacy practices.

  • Firebase Analytics for anonymous usage analytics when enabled by the user.
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and app stability monitoring.
  • Firebase Remote Config to deliver app configuration values and in-app messaging behavior.
  • RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, purchases, and entitlement status.
  • Google Mobile Ads to deliver rewarded ad experiences where available.

We aim to avoid sending sensitive financial details, free-text content, or unnecessary high-detail personal information through analytics and crash-reporting tools.

Permissions and Optional Features

  • Notifications may be used for reminders and scheduled alerts.
  • Biometric access may be used if you enable app lock with your device's supported authentication method.
  • File and media access may be used when you import, export, restore, or attach images inside the app.

You can control many of these permissions through your device settings.

Retention and Your Choices

  • Most finance data entered into Fiscora remains on your device until you edit or delete it.
  • You can remove the app from your device, change permissions, or adjust certain privacy-related settings inside the app.
  • Third-party providers may retain technical records according to their own retention policies.

Age and Use by Minors

Fiscora is intended for users who are at least 16 years old. If you believe a person under 16 has provided personal information to us directly, contact us and we will review the request.

Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect the app and the information processed through it. No method of storage, transmission, or device security is guaranteed to be completely secure, and you remain responsible for protecting access to your device and any files you export.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page. Your continued use of Fiscora after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

If you have a privacy question or request related to Fiscora, contact Rejuvenize at rejuvenize@proton.me.
